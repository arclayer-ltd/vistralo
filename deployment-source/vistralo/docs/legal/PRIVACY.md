# Vistralo privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Vistralo collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers the Vistralo web app at https://app.vistralo.com, the website https://vistralo.com and the Vistralo Screen Recorder extension for Chrome.

## Who runs Vistralo

Vistralo is run by Rezan Ferdous, an individual, in Enayetpur, Sirajganj, Bangladesh. In this policy "Vistralo", "we" and "us" mean Rezan Ferdous.

Email: [hello@arclayer.co](mailto:hello@arclayer.co). Write to this address for anything about your privacy or your account.

We are responsible for your account information and for the records we keep to run the service. For the recordings and files you put into Vistralo, you decide what to record and upload, and we handle them only to provide the service to you.

## The short version

- We keep your account, your projects and the files in them so that you can make, edit and share walkthroughs.
- Every project, with all its recordings, uploads and finished films, is deleted automatically 3 months after the project is made. Download anything you want to keep before then.
- Nobody at Vistralo watches your recordings.
- We send parts of a project to OpenAI only when you ask for AI narration.
- We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and there are no analytics or tracking tools in the app, the website or the extension.

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address, your password (stored by our database provider only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back), and the name and workspace name you enter in your profile.

**What you put into Vistralo.** Your projects and everything in them: screen recordings, videos and screenshots you upload, project names and folders, posters, scripts, generated voice audio, captions and finished films. A recording can show anything that was on your screen, including other people's names, faces, voices or messages. Please record only what you have the right to record.

**Websites you ask us to read.** For a website project, our processing server opens the public web address you give it in a browser, takes pictures of the page and notes which libraries, fonts and effects it uses.

**Your own AI key, if you add one.** If you save an OpenAI key in Settings, your browser locks it with our processing server's public key before sending it, so we store only the locked version. Only the processing server can unlock it, and only to run your jobs.

**Share links.** When you share a project, we create a link with an expiry date. Anyone who has the link can watch the finished video or read the visual brief until the link expires or you revoke it. Your other files stay private.

**Technical information.** To keep you signed in, the app stores your sign-in session in your browser. It also remembers display choices, such as the theme and whether the side bar is collapsed. Our hosting providers record basic details of each request to the app, such as your IP address, browser type, the address requested and the time. Our processing server keeps a log of job numbers, timings and error messages, not the content of your recordings.

**When you write to us.** Your email address and what you write.

**Payments.** Vistralo does not take payments yet. If paid plans start, a payment provider will handle card and billing details, and we will update this policy before that happens.

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

## Why we use it

The law in some places (such as the European Union and the United Kingdom) asks us to say which legal reason we rely on. Here they are, in plain words.

- **To provide Vistralo to you:** to create your account, store your projects, record, process, narrate and render your films, and share them when you ask. Reason: we need to, to provide the service you signed up for.
- **To keep the service safe and working:** to sign you in, stop misuse, fix errors and keep it running. Reason: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service.
- **To make AI narration when you ask for it:** to send the selected pictures, page notes and script text to OpenAI. Reason: we need to, to provide the feature you asked for.
- **To answer you:** to reply to support and privacy requests. Reason: our legitimate interest in helping you, and our legal duties when you use a privacy right.
- **To follow the law:** to keep records or respond to lawful requests when we must. Reason: legal obligation.

We do not use your recordings or projects to train AI models, and we do not sell or rent your information.

## Nobody at Vistralo watches your recordings

People at Vistralo do not view your recordings or projects. The only exceptions are when you ask us to (for example, to help with a problem you report), when it is needed to investigate a security problem or abuse, or when the law requires it.

## AI narration (OpenAI)

AI narration is optional. Only when you ask for it, our processing server sends OpenAI:

- still pictures and small moving-picture frames taken from your recording, and the notes from a website read, so that OpenAI can write a line for each screen;
- the script you approve, so that OpenAI can turn it into a spoken voice.

If you added your own OpenAI key, these requests run on your own OpenAI account. Otherwise they run on Vistralo's account, where your plan includes AI narration. OpenAI says that by default it does not use information sent through its API to train its models, and that it may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse. See OpenAI's own terms for the details: https://openai.com/policies/

## Vistralo for Chrome (the browser extension)

The Vistralo Screen Recorder extension records what you choose and hands the recording to your own Vistralo account.

**What it handles.** Only what you start recording: the video of the tab, window or screen you choose, the tab's or computer's sound if you allow it, and your microphone and camera if you turn them on. With each recording it keeps the title and address of the page it was started on, its length and whether it was interrupted. It also keeps your recording choices (what to record, which microphone and camera, countdown, bubble size) and the state of the recording under way.

**Where it is kept.** On your own computer, in the extension's storage in Chrome. A recording is written there every second so that a crash does not lose it, and it stays there until you save, download or discard it. Removing the extension deletes that storage.

**When it leaves your browser.** Only when you save a recording to Vistralo (with "Save to Vistralo", or by stopping with "Open the recording in Vistralo when I stop" turned on). The extension then opens https://app.vistralo.com in the same browser and hands the recording to the web app there. The web app uploads it to the account you are signed in to, and from then on it is handled like any other project, as this policy describes. The address of the recorded page goes with it, without the part after a "?" or "#". Once the web app has its copy, the extension deletes its own. "Download" saves a file to your computer and sends nothing.

**What it does not do.** The extension has no account or sign-in of its own and holds no password, session or token. It makes no network requests of its own (on a website that blocks the camera, the camera bubble gets its picture from the extension over a connection that stays inside your computer), loads no code from outside its package, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not read the contents of web pages, your browsing history or other tabs. It uses the microphone and camera only while recording, only the ones you choose, and only after Chrome has asked your permission.

**Access to websites.** When installed, the extension can act only on a tab where you click it in the toolbar, to record that tab and show its recording controls there. If you tick "Keep the controls on every site I visit while recording", Chrome asks you for access to all sites. That access is used only to show the controls on other pages during a recording, and unticking the option removes it. Its one script that runs without a click runs only on app.vistralo.com, to hand recordings to the web app.

**How long it is kept.** In your browser: until you save, download or discard the recording, or remove the extension. In your Vistralo account: like any other project, it is deleted automatically 3 months after the project is made.

**Chrome Web Store Limited Use.** The use of information received from the Vistralo Chrome extension adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies), including the [Limited Use](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use) requirements. In particular, data from the extension is used only to provide your recordings and walkthroughs. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide that service (the providers listed below), to comply with the law or to protect against abuse, and not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending. People at Vistralo do not view your recordings unless you ask them to, or it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

## How long we keep it

- **Projects and their files:** deleted automatically 3 months after the project is made, with all its recordings, uploads, frames, scripts, audio and finished films. Editing, re-recording, sharing or moving a project to Trash does not change that date, and a copy of a project keeps the date of the original. You can delete a project sooner by emptying it from Trash. The app shows each project's deletion date.
- **Backups:** our database provider keeps daily backups for 7 days, so a deleted project can remain in a backup for up to 7 more days before it is gone.
- **Working copies on the processing server:** only while a job is running.
- **Share links:** until they expire (after 1, 7 or 30 days, as you choose), you revoke them, or the project is deleted.
- **A deletion record:** when a project is deleted, we keep a short note of it (its ID, dates and how many files were removed), with no names or content.
- **Your account:** until you ask us to close it. We then delete your account and all your projects within 30 days.
- **Logs:** our database provider keeps its request logs for 7 days. Vercel keeps request logs for a short time under its own policy. Our processing server keeps its log for up to 1 month.
- **Emails you send us:** as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 12 months after the conversation ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

## How we protect it

Your connection to Vistralo is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each account reach only its own projects and files. Your own AI key is stored only in its locked form. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities when the law requires it.
